# 📸 Our Memory Lane — A Digital Scrapbook for Anvesha 💖

A romantic, interactive digital scrapbook web application crafted with love for **Anvesha**.

---

## ✨ Features Included

1. **🎀 Multiple Aesthetics in One Click (Theme Switcher)**:
   - **🎀 Soft Pink**: Pinterest-style blush pink, polaroids, washi tape, cute stickers, and handwritten font vibes.
   - **🌌 Dreamy Galaxy**: Deep celestial purple cosmos with glowing stars, neon hearts, and shooting star particles.
   - **🤍 Minimal & Premium**: Champagne cream, warm gold accents, editorial typography, and understated luxury.

2. **📖 The Story of How We Met**:
   - Chapter cards recounting the first glance, the first conversation, and the moment you knew.
   - All text is directly editable on the webpage.

3. **📸 Interactive Polaroid Scrapbook**:
   - Realistic 3D tilt angles, washi tape pins, and photo captions.
   - **Flip to Read**: Click any polaroid to flip it and read the secret handwritten note on the back.
   - **Click to Zoom**: Fullscreen romantic lightbox view.
   - **Upload Custom Polaroids**: Drag-and-drop or select real photos from your computer to add new polaroids instantly.

4. **🤣 Funniest Texts & Inside Jokes**:
   - iMessage/WhatsApp style mockup with funny banter between you and Anvesha.
   - Inside joke cards with punchlines and accuracy ratings.

5. **⏳ Special Dates & Milestones (Memory Timeline)**:
   - Chronological journey through your relationship milestones with animated heart pulse indicators.

6. **✨ “Remember This Day?” Interactive Reveal**:
   - Mystery memory scratch card that uncovers random cherished moments at the tap of a button.
   - Includes a shuffle button to cycle through warm memories.

7. **💌 Love Letters for Anvesha (Wax-Sealed Envelopes)**:
   - Interactive envelope cards:
     - *"Why I Love You"*
     - *"Open When You Miss Me"*
     - *"Open When You Need A Smile"*
   - Clicking an envelope opens air-mail postal stationery with a personal love letter.

8. **🎵 Built-in Romantic Melody & Animations**:
   - Zero-dependency **Web Audio API** romantic Lo-Fi / Harp & Piano synthesizer that plays cozy melodies directly in the browser without broken audio links.
   - Interactive spinning vinyl disc floating widget with play/pause controls.
   - Ambient floating hearts and celestial stars canvas with interactive mouse sparkle trail.

9. **✈️ Future Bucket List**:
   - Checkable adventure items with a live dynamic progress bar counter.
   - Custom input to add new future dreams and travel destinations.

10. **🎁 The Grand Final Surprise Vault**:
    - Locked heart vault at the end of the journey.
    - Unlocking triggers a **full-screen confetti and glowing hearts shower**.
    - Displays a **framed centerpiece photo** of you two (customizable by uploading your real photo).
    - Contains the **hidden final love letter** addressed to Anvesha.
    - Includes a **"Save as Keepsake PDF"** printable button.

## 🚀 How to Run & View the Website

### Option 1: Direct Double Click
Simply double-click [`index.html`](file:///C:/Users/Arindam/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/our-memory-lane/index.html) in your file explorer to open it immediately in Google Chrome, Edge, Safari, or any browser!

### Option 2: Run a Local Web Server
Open PowerShell in this directory:
```powershell
python -m http.server 3000
```
Then visit:
[http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000)

## 💝 How to Personalize for Anvesha
- **Change Photos**: Click *"Add New Polaroid"* or *"Upload Our Real Photo"* on the final surprise frame.
- **Set Your Exact Anniversary**: Use the date picker right below the Days Counter in the hero section.
- **Edit Text & Letters**: Click any story section or letter to edit the text directly on screen.
